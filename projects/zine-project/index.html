Why this song

Although Kanye West is known as a very controversial artist, without Kanye, I wouldn't appreciate music like I do right now. Runaway is the first song that I listened to where I realized music is a true and one of the most art-forms. The pacing of the song and beautiful production by Kanye, as well as the story behind the song impacted me greatly. Every second and every note means so much to me and there is no imperfections, and the creativity of the song is something I don't think can be replicated. I can credit a big chunk of my love for music to Runaway.
